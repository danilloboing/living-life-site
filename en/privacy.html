Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Who we are

Living Life is a single-player life simulation game made by Danillo Boing, an independent developer in Brazil.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no account and no sign-in. It does not collect, send, sell or share personal data, and it contains no analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

What stays on your device

Your lives, graves, family legacy and settings (such as the language) are saved only on your device, in the game's own storage. Your phone may include this storage in its regular backups (iCloud or Google), according to your device settings.

Deleting your data

Open More ▸ Settings ▸ Delete all data in the game, or uninstall the app. Either one erases everything the game saved.

Children

The game is meant for players aged 18 and over. Since it collects no data from anyone, it collects none from children.

This website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may record technical data, such as your IP address, when you visit it, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes

If a future version adds features that handle data, such as ads or purchases, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.

Contact
