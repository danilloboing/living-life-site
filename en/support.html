Support

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Contact

Found a bug, a strange sentence or have an idea? Write to danillo.boing.souza@gmail.com. Tell us your device, the game version (More ▸ Settings ▸ About) and the language you play in.

Changing the language

Open More ▸ Settings and pick English, Português or Español. The whole game switches at once.

Starting over

To erase every life, grave and legacy, open More ▸ Settings ▸ Delete all data and confirm twice. This cannot be undone.

Lost saves

Saved games live only on your device. If you delete the app or change phones without a backup, they cannot be recovered.
